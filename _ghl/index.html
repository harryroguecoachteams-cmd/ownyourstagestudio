Most experts are not short of expertise.

They have years of it. Client results behind them, a reputation that holds inside their own network, referrals that keep arriving. The calendar stays full enough. And outside that circle, almost nobody has heard of them.

That is not a credibility problem. It is a distribution problem, and it does not get solved by posting more often. Being good at the work and being known for the work are two different jobs, and only one of them is the one you trained for.

Who this is not for: anyone new to their field, still building a first offer, or hoping an event will stand in for experience they have not had yet. The host carries the room. That only works when there is something behind it.

What the room actually looks like.

Every event runs on the studio's own stage. A holding slide while people arrive, a framed nameplate for the host and one for each panelist. The audience should not be able to tell it was a video call.

Panelist frames match it, each with their own name and title. All of it is built and checked before the rehearsal, so nothing is being decided on the night.
