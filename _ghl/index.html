Division of responsibilities

What we hold, and what stays with you.

We would rather you read this now than discover it later. We build and produce everything. Promotion is yours, because it is your audience that makes the room.

Own Your Stage Studio handles

  • Event strategy and authority positioning
  • Topic and talking point development
  • Panelist recruitment, screening and management
  • Speaker preparation and coordination
  • Complete event branding
  • Promotional graphics, swipe copy and the registration page
  • Technology and live production
  • Event recording and authority building content
  • Post-event visibility strategy

You handle

  • Completing the intake materials on time
  • Participating in strategy and preparation sessions
  • Reviewing and approving event materials
  • Promoting the event consistently to your audience
  • Sharing the promotional materials we provide
  • Personally inviting relevant contacts
  • Generating registrations
  • Showing up prepared to lead the room

We create the promotional materials. We do not advertise the event, post on your behalf, email your list or guarantee registration or attendance numbers. Those results come from your audience and your panelists, which is why we make the assets easy to send.
