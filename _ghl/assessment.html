The entry point

The Own Your Stage Readiness Assessment

Eight questions. They place you on the visibility ladder and tell you what your next move should be, which is usually not the move most people are planning.

Around three minutes. No email required to see your result.

What it measures

Five factors decide whether expertise turns into recognition.

Positioning, recognition, consistency, audience reach and readiness to hold a room. Nothing here measures how good you are at your work. That is assumed.

Question 1 of 8 · Positioning

If someone in your industry described you in one sentence, would they name a subject?

Question 2 of 8 · Recognition

Where does new business come from?

Question 3 of 8 · Consistency

How consistently does your expertise show up in public?

Question 4 of 8 · Audience reach

If you invited your audience to something next month, who would actually come?

Question 5 of 8 · Platform

Do you have a platform of your own, or do you appear on other people's?

Question 6 of 8 · Assets

If someone asked for video of you speaking, what could you send today?

Question 7 of 8 · Network

Could you name five credible experts who would share a stage with you?

Question 8 of 8 · Readiness

What has been stopping you from doing something like this already?
