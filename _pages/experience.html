Division of responsibilities

Where our work ends and yours begins.

This is the part most studios leave to the small print. It is on this page because the engagement only works when both sides know it before signing.

We handle

  • Event strategy and authority positioning
  • Topic and talking point development
  • Panelist recruitment, screening and management
  • Speaker preparation and coordination
  • Event branding and every promotional asset
  • Registration page and supporting assets
  • Technology and live production
  • Event recording and post-event content

We do not handle

  • Advertising the event
  • Posting promotional content on your behalf
  • Emailing your list or your panelists' lists
  • Personally inviting attendees
  • Generating registrations
  • Guaranteeing registration or attendance numbers
  • Publishing your post-event content for you

We create the promotional materials. You and your panelists send them. The room is built from your combined audiences, which is also why the panel is chosen for reach as well as expertise.
