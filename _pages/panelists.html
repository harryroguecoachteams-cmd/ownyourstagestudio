The terms, stated plainly

What is asked of you in return.

A panel works because every expert in it brings a room. These are the commitments, in full, before you apply.

You commit to

  • A $47 non-refundable commitment and administrative fee
  • Completing all speaker information by the deadline
  • Preparing a roughly ten minute educational presentation
  • Promoting the event at least three times beforehand
  • Sharing the promotional materials we provide
  • Attending the preparation session and the technical check
  • Appearing live, on camera, from a quiet environment

The presentation is not

  • A sales presentation or a pitch
  • A request for payment from attendees
  • Repeated promotion of paid products or services
  • A prerecorded video, an avatar or a stand-in
  • A guarantee of financial or business outcomes

The fee is not a speaking fee and buys nothing except a reserved position. It offsets onboarding, coordination, graphics, technical setup and speaker support. It is non-refundable because that work begins the moment your place is confirmed. If we cancel the event without offering a rescheduled date you accept, it is refunded in full.
